## About

IRIS Energy is a French company and registered brand established in 2011. Its target markets cover Europe and Mediterranean countries, with products serving customers across Europe, Africa and the Middle East.

IRIS Energy has operated in the Egyptian market since 2020 through cooperation with AOI-ABD. This local presence combines product expertise with engineering support for project studies, installation, maintenance and long-term service.

We accompany clients from early project stages through system selection, technical studies, installation and ongoing support. Our objective is to propose a solution that balances performance, budget, energy efficiency and service quality.

## Solutions

### Swimming Pools & Spas

IRIS heat-pump systems provide heating and cooling for private pools, hotel pools, schools, clubs and spa applications. System selection should consider pool volume, target temperature, ambient conditions, operating season and pool-cover use.

### Commercial Pools & Industrial Basins

Large-capacity and multi-unit systems can be configured for commercial and industrial water applications. The engineering study determines capacity, hydraulic integration, controls and redundancy requirements.

### Domestic Hot Water

Air-to-water heat-pump technology can support efficient domestic hot-water generation for suitable residential and commercial applications.

### Building Heating & Cooling

IRIS Energy solutions can be applied to residential building heating and cooling where system design, emitters and water temperatures are compatible with heat-pump operation.

### Pool Covers & Rollers

Thermal pool covers help reduce heat loss, limit contamination and reduce chemical demand. Available covers may be adapted to different pool shapes, colours and landscape requirements, with roller systems for easier handling.
